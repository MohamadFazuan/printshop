Soalan biasa

Questions

Bagaimana saya dapat kunci lesen?How do I get a licence key?

Tekan Beli di atas untuk WhatsApp kami. Bayar melalui pindahan bank atau DuitNow, hantar resit, dan kunci dihantar biasanya dalam 1 hari bekerja. Buka Printshop, tampal kunci pada skrin pertama, siap.

Press Buy above to WhatsApp us. Pay by bank transfer or DuitNow, send the receipt, and the key usually arrives within 1 working day. Open Printshop, paste the key on the first screen, and you're done.

Boleh cuba dulu sebelum beli?Can I try it before I buy?

Tiada versi percuma, tapi kami boleh tunjuk demo secara langsung di WhatsApp atau panggilan video: taip idea, pratonton garis potong, eksport helaian. Semua jualan muktamad, jadi tanya apa sahaja dulu.

There is no free version, but we will show you a live demo over WhatsApp or a video call: type an idea, preview the cut line, export a sheet. All sales are final, so ask anything first.

Perlu langganan ChatGPT?Do I need a ChatGPT plan?

Ya. Imej dijana oleh Codex pada pelan ChatGPT anda sendiri, yang dibayar terus kepada OpenAI. Harga Printshop tidak termasuk langganan itu.

Yes. Artwork is generated by Codex on your own ChatGPT plan, paid directly to OpenAI. The Printshop price does not include it.

Boleh guna di lebih dari satu komputer?Can I use it on more than one computer?

Basic dan Pro untuk 1 komputer setiap satu. Untuk beberapa komputer atau cawangan, pilih pakej Khas. Tukar komputer baharu? WhatsApp kami dan kami pindahkan lesen anda.

Basic and Pro cover 1 computer each. For several computers or branches, choose Custom. Replacing your computer? WhatsApp us and we will move your licence.

Perlu bayar API OpenAI?Do I pay for an OpenAI API?

Tidak. Printshop guna Codex CLI pada pelan ChatGPT yang anda sudah ada. Tiada kunci API disimpan dalam aplikasi, dan tiada caj per imej.

No. Printshop uses Codex CLI on the ChatGPT plan you already have. No API key is stored in the app, and there is no per-image charge.

Perlu internet?Does it need the internet?

Menjana imej perlukan internet kerana Codex berjalan melalui akaun ChatGPT anda. Besarkan AI, garis potong dan eksport PDF berjalan dalam komputer, luar talian.

Generating artwork does, because Codex runs through your ChatGPT account. AI upscaling, cut lines and PDF export run on your computer, offline.

Mesin potong saya boleh baca garis potong?Will my cutter read the cut line?

Garis ialah warna spot bernama CutContour, ditetapkan overprint. Itu nama yang dicari VersaWorks, Mimaki, Summa, Graphtec Cutting Master dan Flexi. Tanda daftar ditambah oleh perisian mesin anda. Cetak satu helai ujian dulu sebelum run besar.

The line is a spot colour named CutContour, set to overprint. That is the name VersaWorks, Mimaki, Summa, Graphtec Cutting Master and Flexi look for. Registration marks are added by your cutter software. Run one test sheet before a big job.

Boleh buat banner besar?Can it do large banners?

Boleh minta apa-apa saiz, dan bentuk kertas sentiasa betul. Tapi resolusi ada had: AI upscale sehingga 4×. Untuk banner 4 × 10 kaki, guna hasilnya sebagai konsep atau latar. Aplikasi akan beritahu bila saiz tidak sedia cetak.

You can ask for any size and the shape is always right. Resolution has a ceiling: AI upscale goes up to 4×. For a 4 × 10 ft banner, use the result as a concept or background. The app tells you when a size isn't print-ready.

Kenapa macOS atau Windows beri amaran?Why does macOS or Windows show a warning?

Versi semasa belum ditandatangani secara digital. Pada Mac: klik kanan aplikasi, pilih Open, kemudian Open sekali lagi. Pada Windows: More info, kemudian Run anyway. Sekali sahaja.

The current build is not code-signed yet. On a Mac: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. On Windows: More info, then Run anyway. Once only.
